# Comparable public EFL samples

These historical Electricity Facts Labels include the recurring fields supported by the optimizer. They are parser fixtures, not current offers. The default `providers.json` compares two plans in the same CenterPoint service territory.

- `gexa_saver_12_centerpoint.pdf`: Gexa Energy Saver 12, CenterPoint, dated May 16, 2025.
- `gexa_saver_24_centerpoint.pdf`: Gexa Energy Saver 24, CenterPoint, dated May 16, 2025.
- `gexa_24_aep_north.pdf`: extra Gexa 24 fixture for AEP North; excluded from the default comparison.
- `gexa_basic_plus_12_aep_north.pdf`: extra usage-credit fixture for AEP North; excluded from the default comparison.
